What jobmcp does, and what it doesn’t

jobmcp does not apply for you. Your agent does, from your side, on the company’s own form.

jobmcp does

  • Collects open roles from employers’ own career pages, so you see them when the company posts them.
  • Matches them to the titles, places and keywords you choose.
  • Gives your agent the matches and the company’s apply link.
  • Keeps a plain record of what your agent applied to, so nothing is applied for twice.

jobmcp doesn’t

  • Apply to anything. There is no “apply for me” button, on purpose.
  • Ask for or store your résumé, phone number, address, name or answers to application questions.
  • Read your email, or sign in to other sites as you.
  • Hold anything worth selling: we keep your email, your search preferences, your saved jobs and a status per job.

How it works

  1. Create an account

    An email and a password. Then say what you’re looking for: job titles, places, remote or not, and the most applications you want in a day.

  2. Connect your agent

    Create a personal token in your portal and paste one command into Claude, Codex or Gemini. The token opens your list and nobody else’s. Revoke it whenever you like.

  3. Ask your agent to apply

    It reads your matches, claims a job, opens the company’s form and fills it in with the résumé and details you keep on your side. It asks you about anything it doesn’t know, such as visa sponsorship, disability, gender or a company’s own questions, and never guesses. If it can’t finish a form, it skips the job and says why.

  4. Check the record

    Every job your agent touches appears in your portal as applied, skipped or failed.

Questions

Where does my résumé go?

Not to jobmcp. Keep it as a file your agent can read, or in your agent’s own project or memory. When your agent submits a company’s form, the résumé goes to that company, exactly as if you had applied yourself.

What does jobmcp know about me?

Your email address, a hash of your password, your search preferences, the jobs you saved, and a status for each job your agent worked on. The privacy page lists everything.

Can my agent apply to any job?

Only where it can complete the form. Some companies require an account or show a captcha. Your agent records those as skipped or failed, and you can open them yourself from your portal.

What stops an agent from applying to hundreds of jobs?

You set a daily limit in your preferences, 20 by default. Once you reach it, jobmcp won’t let your agent start another application that day, and it never lets it apply to the same job twice.

Where do the jobs come from?

From employers’ own career pages, on hiring platforms such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable and Workday. These are the companies’ own listings, not copies from a job board.

How do I delete my data?

In your portal, open Account and choose Delete account. Your preferences, saved jobs, application record and tokens are erased immediately.
