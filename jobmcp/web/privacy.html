What we store

jobmcp is built so your résumé and contact details never reach it. This page lists everything it does hold.

What we store about you

  • Your email address, to sign you in.
  • A hash of your password. We can’t read your password.
  • Your search preferences: the titles, places, keywords and limits you set.
  • Saved jobs: the ids of jobs you bookmarked.
  • An application record: for each job your agent worked on, a status (claimed, applied, skipped, failed), the time, and an optional short note your agent wrote.
  • Your agent tokens: stored as hashes, with the name you gave them and when they were last used.

What we never store

  • Your résumé or cover letters.
  • Your name, phone number, address or links to your profiles.
  • Your answers to application questions.
  • Your email contents, or logins to any other site.

There is no field for any of this. If you want to keep your agent’s notes free of personal details, ask it to keep them short.

What your agent does

jobmcp does not apply for you. Your agent applies on the company’s own page, using the résumé and details you gave it. That information goes to the company you’re applying to, and to your agent’s provider according to that provider’s terms. It does not go to jobmcp.

If an application asks for something your agent doesn’t have, such as visa sponsorship, disability, veteran status, race, gender or a question the company wrote itself, your agent is told to ask you and not to guess, and to let you decline the voluntary self-identification questions. Your answers go to the company on its form. jobmcp never receives them, and a note your agent leaves with the job should only name the topic, not your answer.

Your control

  • Revoke any agent token in your portal and it stops working at once.
  • Delete your account in your portal under Account. Your preferences, saved jobs, application record and tokens are erased immediately.

Cookies and logs

The site sets no cookies. Staying signed in uses one value kept in your browser’s local storage, which signing out removes. Like most web servers, the one running jobmcp may keep ordinary logs of requests, such as time, page and IP address.
